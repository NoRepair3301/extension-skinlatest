## Short description (max 132 characters)
Restyle the page you are on with calm built-in themes or an AI-suggested one. Not affiliated with any website.

## Long description
Skinly AI changes how a page looks, not what it says. Click "Change my page" and a built-in theme is applied within a second. If you added your own AI key and agreed to the consent text, a small description of the page's layout (shapes, sizes and colours) is sent to the AI provider you chose, which suggests colours, spacing and a few layout options. The extension checks the suggestion, builds the styles itself from fixed templates and applies them. Restore original removes everything it added.

What you get
- Free: Calm light, Midnight, High contrast and an Auto theme that follows your browser's light or dark setting.
- Skinly Plus (optional, paid): Ocean, Warm paper and Field themes with their scenes, and your own pictures as side art. Sign in with an email code; the price is shown in Settings. Cancel any time; Plus stays until the end of the paid period.
- Original animated art (scenes) in the empty side space of a page, and five small pets. Art never covers page content.
- Your own image or short video as side art (Skinly Plus). Files stay on your device.
- Optional AI suggestions with your own key (Gemini or Groq). Nothing is sent without your click and consent.
- Remember a theme for a site, or turn on automatic theming for that one site.
- Keyboard shortcuts and a find-a-link palette that only moves focus; you press Enter yourself.
- Privacy blur for parts you pick, and a safe screenshot of the visible area (stays on your computer).
- Table tools: sort, filter and sticky headers on plain data tables, with Restore order.
- Settings in tabs, shortcuts with their real keys, and one button to erase all Skinly data.

What it never does
- It never logs in, fills in, submits or clicks anything for you.
- It never sends page text, links, form values, images, URLs or titles anywhere.
- It has no analytics or ads. An account is needed only for Skinly Plus, and Skinly's server never receives your pages, sites or keys.

Skinly AI is not affiliated with any website or browser vendor.
